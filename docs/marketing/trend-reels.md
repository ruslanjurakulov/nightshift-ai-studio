# Trend templates: Instagram showcase reels

One 15–30 second reel per trend template (migration 0183, the Trends gallery). Each reel shows a real result made in our own
Studio from that template. The structure is the same every time: a **hook** (0–3 s), the **steps** ("made from this template
in 60 s": what the person actually does), the **result**, then the **CTA**.

## Rules for every reel

- **Everything on screen was made in Nightshift Studio** from the template named, with the settings in the template. No stock
  footage, nobody else's video, and no competitor's output.
- **"60 s" means the person's own time** on screen (open Trends, pick the template, fill in the words, press Generate). It never
  means the render time. If a step takes longer in the recording, cut the waiting and caption it `(generating…)`. Do not hide it.
- **No invented numbers**: no view counts, no "10x sales", no customer quotes, and no "made by thousands of creators". The only
  claim is that this result came from this template.
- **Label AI**: every reel's caption carries "Made with AI in Nightshift Studio", and the in-app AI disclosure stays on the export.
- **No prices or costs** in the reel, the caption or the comments. Price questions go to the pricing page.
- People are shown only from behind or as hands, as the templates are written. No real person's face or voice is imitated.
- Captions go in Uzbek, Russian and English (three versions, or one reel with burned-in subtitles in the account's main language).

Shared CTA (pick one per post): **"Open Trends → pick a format → make yours."** · **"Link in bio: Trends."**

## 1. Product ad 9:16 (`product-ad-vertical`)

- **Hook (0–3 s):** the finished hero frame, then freeze: "This product shot took me one sentence."
- **Steps (3–15 s):** screen recording. Trends → "Product ad 9:16" → type `a matte ceramic coffee mug` in {product} and
  `deep teal` in {brand_color} → Generate.
- **Result (15–25 s):** the full 10-second clip.
- **CTA (25–30 s):** "Your product, same format. Trends → Product ad."

## 2. Before / after (`before-after-reveal`)

- **Hook:** the cluttered "before" frame, with the text "wait for it".
- **Steps:** Trends → "Before / after" → describe the before and the after in two lines → Generate.
- **Result:** the wipe reveal, played twice (the second time at half speed).
- **CTA:** "Makeovers, renovations, results. One template."

## 3. Promo poster (`promo-poster-square`)

- **Hook:** the poster slides in: "A launch poster without a designer?"
- **Steps:** Trends → "Promo poster" → {product} `wireless headphones`, {headline} `NEW COLLECTION`, {brand_color}
  `electric orange` → Generate.
- **Result:** the poster full screen, then shown mocked into a feed post.
- **CTA:** "Change three words, get your poster."

## 4. Cinematic intro (`cinematic-intro`)

- **Hook:** the camera rises out of the fog, and the title card lands on the empty sky.
- **Steps:** Trends → "Cinematic intro" → {channel_topic} `a history channel`, {setting} `ancient stone ruins on a mountain
  ridge` → Generate.
- **Result:** the 10-second intro with the channel title added in the editor (show that step: it is honest about where the
  text comes from).
- **CTA:** "Give your channel an opening."

## 5. Faceless story (`faceless-story`)

- **Hook:** a first line in captions over the scene: "The lighthouse keeper had one night left…"
- **Steps:** Trends → "Faceless story" → {story} → Generate. Then add the narration and captions in the editor.
- **Result:** the scene with captions in the lower third that the template keeps clear.
- **CTA:** "Stories without showing your face."

## 6. Podcast clip (`podcast-clip`)

- **Hook:** the waveform pulses: "No studio? This is the studio."
- **Steps:** Trends → "Podcast clip" → {topic} → Generate → drop your own audio clip on top in the editor.
- **Result:** the backdrop playing under a real 10-second audio excerpt **of our own** (never someone else's podcast).
- **CTA:** "Turn an episode into a clip."

## 7. Travel montage (`travel-montage`)

- **Hook:** the drone shot over the skyline: "Samarkand in 10 seconds."
- **Steps:** Trends → "Travel montage" → {destination} `Samarkand, Uzbekistan` → Generate.
- **Result:** the montage set to music from a licensed library.
- **Caption note:** say that the shots are AI-made, not filmed on location.
- **CTA:** "Your city, your rhythm."

## 8. Food reveal (`food-reveal`)

- **Hook:** steam fills the frame: "Lift the lid."
- **Steps:** Trends → "Food reveal" → {dish} `Uzbek plov in a cast-iron kazan` → Generate.
- **Result:** the 5-second reveal, looped twice.
- **Caption note:** the dish shown is AI-made. Do not present it as a specific restaurant's food.
- **CTA:** "A menu teaser in one line."

## 9. Fact short card (`fact-card`)

- **Hook:** the card: "Did you know?" (pause for one second before the fact).
- **Steps:** Trends → "Fact short card" → {fact} `An octopus has three hearts.`, {subject} `an octopus` → Generate.
- **Result:** the card, then three cards in a row made the same way.
- **Caption note:** use only facts you have checked. The template formats the fact; it does not check it.
- **CTA:** "A fact channel's cards in minutes."

## 10. Motivational quote (`quote-poster`)

- **Hook:** the quote fades in over the sunrise: "Small steps, every day."
- **Steps:** Trends → "Motivational quote" → {quote} (your own words) and {scene} → Generate.
- **Result:** the poster, then a second one with a different scene.
- **Caption note:** use your own words or a public-domain quote with its author named. Never present a made-up quote as a real
  person's.
- **CTA:** "Your words, on a poster."
