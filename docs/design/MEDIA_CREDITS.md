# Media credits

Every picture and clip on the public pages is stock photography or footage by a Pexels contributor, used under the
Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/: free use, credit not required, no implying that anyone or anything in a picture endorses
Nightshift, no selling unaltered copies). They are not output of any account; each is shown only as an example of
the kind of picture a video can use, with a visible label ("Example frame (stock photo)", "Example frame (stock footage)", "Example clip (stock footage)", or, on the capability wall, "Stock photos, illustrating each tool") and a credit line ("Photo: name / Pexels"). The credits are read from one
data file, `command-center/lib/site/media.ts`; the footer's collapsed "Image credits" list is built from it, and
`tests/site-media.test.tsx` keeps this table, the data file, the dictionaries and the files in step.

Only derivatives are committed (`command-center/scripts/make-site-media.py`, one shared grade: blacks lifted about 3%, highlights warmed
slightly, saturation capped on the hottest pictures). The originals (clips at 1920 to 2730 px wide, photographs of 3000 to 10404 px) are
not in the repository. Round 8 dropped, because nothing uses them any more: the misty river clip (18197835), the fishermen (39395221), the workshop (19208266), the woven lanterns (16046217), the dawn fog (31550736) and the calm water (14701162).
