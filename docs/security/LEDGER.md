# Security ledger

The single record of every security finding. Process and conventions are in
[README.md](README.md). Newest findings go at the bottom of their section.
A row is never deleted. It moves to `fixed` or `wontfix` with a reason.

**Status:** `open` = hole exists; `fixed` = merged fix and regression test;
`wontfix` = accepted risk, reason in root cause.
**Severity:** critical > high > medium > low > info (rubric in README).
