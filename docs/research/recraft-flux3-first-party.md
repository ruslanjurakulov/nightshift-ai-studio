## Why they are not for sale

Every entry has `terms_gate: terms_review_required` and `api_exposure: web_only`, so `sellable_models()` (migration 0035) never returns it, and no `credit_prices` row exists. `tools/probe_models.py --all` (and the probe workflow with an empty model input) skips all four; name one with `--model` to probe it.

### Recraft (Developer Terms, effective 2026-10-20, `recraft.ai/legal/developer-terms`, read in full 2026-10-25)

- 3.1 / 3.5: a non-sublicensable licence "to develop, test, and operate applications or services that integrate with" Recraft; end users must be bound to an enforceable agreement and acceptable-use policy at least as protective as these terms, with a privacy policy, and violators suspended.
- **3.7 No Substitution:** a service "through which third parties submit prompts ... and receive Assets generated by earlier API calls in place of a new API call" is barred. Every Nightshift generation must be a fresh call: never serve a cached or shared Recraft output for another customer's prompt. The adapter makes one call per submit and never replays; a test pins it. 3.6 allows caching "to improve the performance" of the application, and 3.7 does not restrict storing or selling the individual Assets you own.
- **3.2(x):** you may not use the API or Assets "to develop goods or services that compete with Recraft or the Services". A general image-generation product could be read as that. This is the clause that keeps the gate shut rather than open. 1.4: a separate written reseller or platform agreement with Recraft prevails over these terms; asking for one is the clean way out.
- 6.2: you own the Assets and Recraft assigns its copyright rights, but Assets "may not be used to train any artificial intelligence model" (a breach ends the licence; the duty survives termination), so Nightshift must pass that rule to its users. 6.4: do not remove Recraft's watermarks or machine-readable metadata; follow AI-marking laws. 2.1: the developer must be 18+. No "Powered by" duty was found.
- Not verified: Uzbekistan sign-up and payment; the consumer Terms of Service (`/legal/terms`, 21 October 2026) beyond the research report's reading. The API has no per-end-user id field (OpenAI's `user` is not in the parameter table), so abuse cannot be traced to one end user on Recraft's side.

### FLUX 3 (Developer Terms of Service and FLUX API Service Terms, both last revised 2026-08-04, `bfl.ai/legal/*`, read in full 2026-10-25 for this change; the research report had only the registry's one-line summary)

- API Terms 2(a): the licence is "solely to develop and operate integrations whereby End Users of a Developer Application can interface with the FLUX AI Models from within the Developer Application"; "may not host ... an API endpoint to any FLUX AI Models that allows third parties to integrate" them. So a credits product inside Nightshift's own web application is the licensed shape, and a public pass-through API is not: `web_only`.
- **API Terms 2(b):** the developer grants BFL "a fully paid, royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive, and fully sublicensable right and license to use ... Developer's Input and Output ... Company may use Inputs and Outputs to train and improve its artificial intelligence models". That covers every customer's prompts and uploaded pictures. A licence of that reach needs a privacy-notice and consent decision by the owner, and is why the gate is `terms_review_required`, not open.
- Developer Terms 2(c): end users must be bound to an enforceable agreement, acceptable-use policy and privacy policy "no less protective" than BFL's terms and Usage Policy (API Terms 7: "at least as restrictive"); 6(k): do not upload images, videos or personal data "relating to individuals under the age of 18"; 6(i): never remove Content Credentials (C2PA) or claim Output is human-made; 6(c): no use of Output to train, distil or fine-tune other models; 9: consent of any identifiable person in an Input.
- Developer Terms 3(b) / 4(b): the developer owns the Output and the developer and its end users may use it commercially; it may not be unique across users.
- Usage Policy (revised 2026-08-04, read): no election or political-campaign content, nothing that "falsely represents events, things or people", no tampering with C2PA credentials or watermarks, no military, surveillance or law-enforcement use, and extra EU AI Act limits for EU users.
- Not read: the EU variants of both terms, any Order Form. Uzbekistan access is not verified (the sanctions clause names US-sanctioned jurisdictions).
- **The `flux-2-pro`, `flux-2-max` and `flux-2-flex` entries sit under the same terms and carry `terms_gate: null`.** That is a decision for the owner and is not changed here.

## What the adapters do

### `image.recraft` (`modules/capabilities/recraft.py`)

Sources (`recraft.ai/docs/api-reference/...` as markdown, read 2026-10-25): getting-started, models/{overview,recraft-v4-1,recraft-v4-1-flash}, endpoints, image-inputs-and-results, error-handling, pricing, appendix.

- `POST /v1/images/generations`, synchronous. `response_format: b64_json` and `image_format: png` are always sent, `n` is 1. **The docs print the result link as `https://...` and name no host** (the links live about 24 hours and are public), so no host could go on an allow-list; rather than guess one, nothing is downloaded. The image arrives inline and is stored through `safe_fetch` (type sniffed from the bytes, atomic write). An answer that carries a link instead of bytes is treated as unusable and possibly billed (`UNCONFIRMED SPEND`), and the link is never requested.
- Only the raster 1K models are offered. Vector (`_vector`, $0.08 on V4.1) and Pro 2K (`_pro`, $0.21) are priced apart and refused; so are V4, V4 Styles, V3 and the Utility line. Styles, style references, colour controls and the seed are not wired; V4.1 takes no `negative_prompt`.
- `size` is the job's aspect ratio (eleven plain `w:h` rows of the vendor's size table); with none it is left out and Recraft picks the shape from the prompt. The price does not depend on it.
- Errors: plain text or JSON ("request unauthorized" for a rejected token), so the body is read as text and classified by status and words. A lost submit answer (timeout, dropped connection, 5xx, unreadable 2xx) is `UNCONFIRMED SPEND` with `not_accepted` False; 401/402/404/429 are proof of refusal.
- `credits` in the answer is the units charged ($1 = 1,000 units); it is not recorded yet. Rate limit: 100 images per minute and 5 requests per second per user across all tokens (registry allows 2 per organization).

### `image.bfl3` (`modules/capabilities/bfl.py`)

Sources (`docs.bfl.ml/...` as markdown, read 2026-10-25): api-reference/utility/generate-an-image-with-flux-3 (OpenAPI) and ...-flux-3-klein, flux_3/flux3_image_generate, api_integration/{integration_guidelines,errors}, quick_start/pricing; prices from the structured price list in bfl.ai/pricing.

- Why not the existing `image.bfl` adapter: FLUX 3 "rejects unknown fields with HTTP 422" (no `width`, `height`, `seed`), takes `aspect_ratio` + `resolution`, and is priced by that `resolution`. The new adapter subclasses it for the key, `x-key` header, base URL and the rule that the polling URL is the task id and must stay on `bfl.ai`. FLUX.2 behaviour is unchanged.
- `POST /v1/flux-3-image` or `/v1/flux-3-image-klein` answers `{id, polling_url, cost, input_mp, output_mp}`; `GET <polling_url>` answers `Pending` / `Reasoning` / `Generating` / `Ready` / `Request Moderated` / `Content Moderated` / `Error` / `Task not found`. `Ready` carries `result.sample`, a signed link on `delivery.<region>.bfl.ai` ("do not rely on any specific hostname", "whitelist the wildcard `delivery.*.bfl.ai`"), valid 1 hour per the quickstart and 10 minutes per the integration guide. The allow-list is the domain `bfl.ai`; the key is never sent to the link and a redirect is never followed.
- **"A failed task can come back as HTTP 503 with a normal JSON body."** The shared `_call` gained an optional `ok_statuses` argument (default empty, so every other adapter is unchanged); the poll reads the body's status first and treats a 503 without a known status as a real outage (`unavailable`, poll again).
- **The format of the result is not documented** (FLUX 3 Image has no output-format field; the docs' example saves `.png`), so `safe_fetch` gained a pseudo type `image/*`: the first bytes decide among PNG / JPEG / WebP, the file is stored under that extension, anything else is refused and removed.
- `resolution` (the priced tier) is always sent from the job's image size and a job with none is refused; the vendor default is `1k`. Offered: 1K and 2K. `768sq` and `1.5k` have no name in the registry's size list, and `4k` costs $0.607 (12 times 1K). `grounding` (web and image search for the prompt) is ON by default on FLUX 3 Image and is sent as `false`; the pages state no price for it and a customer's prompt is not sent to a search engine. Klein has no such field. `safety_tolerance` stays the vendor default (2). The opaque per-organization id goes in `user`.
- Moderation: `Request Moderated` (before generating) and `Content Moderated` (the finished image withheld) are `policy`. BFL does not say whether either is billed, so every failure message ends with that caveat and none claims the task was free. A failure after BFL said `Ready` (bad link, bad file, download error) starts with `SPEND MAY HAVE OCCURRED`.
- Not wired: editing and up to ten reference images, bounding-box layouts (they are just prompt text and would pass), FLUX 3 Video and Video Edit/Upscale, the FLUX.2 family, webhooks, regional endpoints (`api.eu.bfl.ai`, `api.us.bfl.ai`; the polling URL already names the region).

### The owner's key checker (merged from main during this change)

`command-center/lib/model-keys.ts` (docs/MODEL_API_KEYS.md) lists one entry per provider of the model registry, and its test fails for a provider with no entry, so Recraft got one: `GET https://external.api.recraft.ai/v1/users/me` with `Authorization: Bearer` (Recraft's "Get user information", "including credits balance": a free read that makes nothing), `RECRAFT_API_TOKEN`, key page `app.recraft.ai/profile/api`. The answer's fields are not documented, so only "a JSON object" counts as an answer. The card counts in three tests went from 13 to 14. Nothing was called with a key.

## To enable one (owner)

1. Recraft: decide 3.2(x) and 3.7 in writing, ideally by asking Recraft for a reseller or platform agreement (1.4); put the no-AI-training rule and the end-user terms of 3.5 into Nightshift's own terms. BFL: decide the training-licence question (2(b)), the privacy notice, the under-18 upload rule and the end-user flow-down; consider whether the FLUX.2 entries need the same gate.
2. Create the key (`RECRAFT_API_TOKEN` for Recraft; `BFL_API_KEY` is already wired), set the repository secret (never in chat) and a spend cap; check Uzbekistan sign-up and payment.
3. `python tools/probe_models.py --dry-run --model recraft-v4.1-flash`, then the real probe by naming it (about $0.007 for Recraft Flash, $0.035 V4.1, $0.009 Klein, $0.048 FLUX 3 Image).
4. The probe is also what shows the real delivery host and the format FLUX 3 serves. Re-check the prices, set `confirmed: true`, add the `credit_prices` rows, and clear `terms_gate` in a reviewed change.
