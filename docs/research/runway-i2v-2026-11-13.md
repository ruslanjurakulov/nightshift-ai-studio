# Runway image-to-video for an operator-only trial, 2026-11-13

Status: registry, adapter and Studio change plus tests. Nothing goes on sale. No vendor API was called and no key was used.
No migration: the operator preview is migration 0139, already on main.

The owner decided on 2026-11-13 to start a "one photo -> 4 styled videos" feature. Step 1 is letting the platform operator try
Runway image-to-video in the operator's own credits-exempt organization (0139), and nobody else. Both Runway video models stay
`hidden` in the database and get no new credit price in this change.

## What changed

- **Registry** (`schemas/model_registry.json`, `runway-gen4.5` and `runway-gen4-turbo`):
  - `operator_preview: true` on both.
  - `api_documented` now points at the API reference of this date.
  - Pricing restates the page with `as_of` 2026-11-13 and stays `confirmed: false`. The figure comes from the page, not from a bill.
  - `terms_notes` gained the training licence, the Enterprise alternative, the age rule, the photo-consent rule, the embargo and the attribution requirement.
  - `durations_s` stays 5 and 10: conservative, inside Runway's 2 to 10, and enough for the feature.
  - The upscaler (`runway-video-upscale`) is not part of the trial and is unchanged.
- **The gen4.5 probe is image-to-video now.** It used to probe text-to-video, but the feature needs the i2v path proven. The new probe
  is the cheapest valid call: a 5 s clip of a drawn 1280x720 frame at 16:9, the generated picture `tools/probe_models.py` already draws
  for i2v probes. Runway bills by the second, not by the ratio, and 16:9 sends that frame uncropped. `--all` behaves as before: it skips
  terms-gated models and models past their shutdown date. Both Runway entries are ungated, so `--all` probes them, as it did before this
  change. Availability (`hidden`) lives in the database, and the probe tool has never read it.
- **The adapter** (`modules/capabilities/video.py`, `RunwayAdapter`) already matched the endpoint, the version header and the six
  ratios. Four things were fixed:
  - A length outside 2 to 10 whole seconds is refused before any call. It used to be sent anyway.
  - `ASSET.INVALID` is `bad_request`. It used to be `unavailable`, which is retryable, so the same unusable picture would have been paid for again.
  - A 4xx whose body names a credit shortfall is `quota`. A 400 used to read as "change your input". Runway's error page does not
    document these words, so the match is narrow on purpose (see "Not verified").
  - A status the docs do not list is logged and kept pending, as the Vidu adapter does, rather than silently treated as running.
  - THROTTLED is pinned as still running.
  - The probe prints Runway's own `cost.credits` beside OK, so the per-second figure can be compared with a real bill.
- **The Studio** shows a model's `attribution` with a link: beside the price key in the panel, every desk and the overview composer, and
  on the job card of what that model made or is making. Before this change the line appeared only on the Models catalog, so a Runway
  clip made in the Studio carried no "Powered by Runway". The words are Runway's and are not translated. The spoken label around them is
  in en, ru and uz.

## Open question: third-party models through Runway

Runway's API reference of this date also lists other makers' models on the same `image_to_video` endpoint, paid in Runway credits:
Kling (`kling3.0_*`, `klingO3_*`, `kling2.5_turbo_pro`, `kling_4`), Seedance (`seedance2*`), Veo (`veo3.1`, `veo3.1_fast`),
MiniMax Hailuo (`hailuo3*`), Wan, Grok Imagine and Gemini Omni. Some of these are terms-gated or `web_only` here under their own makers'
terms. Nobody has confirmed whether Runway's Terms of Use alone cover reselling them inside our app, or whether each maker's own terms
still apply through Runway. **Get Runway's written confirmation before any of them is wired or sold through Runway.** Nothing in this
change uses them.

## Before a sale (none of this is done here)

1. **Training on uploads.** Either a privacy-notice line saying that Runway may train on what a person uploads and on the clips it
   makes (Terms §4.4), or the Enterprise Terms (§5.2), which forbid that training. This is the owner's choice.
2. **Photo consent.** Before an upload is sent to Runway, the person confirms in the UI that the photo is theirs, or that everyone in
   it agreed (§4.2). This needs a UI step in en, ru and uz, and a server-side record of the confirmation.
3. **Age rule.** Our user terms carry 13+, and under 18 only with a parent's or guardian's permission (§2.2).
4. **Embargo.** Decide the `blocked_countries` list for §18.12. The terms name none, so none is set.
5. **Price.** The owner sets the credit price. The probe's reported `cost.credits` is compared with 12 and 5 credits a second first.
   Then `pricing.confirmed` is set to `true`, and availability moves to `beta` in a reviewed change. A `credit_prices` row for these
   units may already exist from `docs/sql/prices_2026_10_01.sql`. Check it, and do not assume that it is the sale price.
6. **Attribution beyond the Studio.** The Library and an exported video do not carry the line yet. A job card loses it once the
   model is no longer offered to that viewer, because the line is read from the offered models' spec. Recording the attribution on
   the job's result would make it permanent.
7. **A refused input is still billed.** Runway does not refund `SAFETY.INPUT.*`. The job releases the person's hold, so we pay the
   vendor cost of every refused photo. Price that in, or screen inputs first.

## Owner steps for the trial

1. Merge. Then run **Actions -> Probe models -> Run workflow** twice: once with `model` = `runway-gen4-turbo` (5 s at 5 credits a
   second, about $0.25) and once with `runway-gen4.5` (5 s i2v at 12 credits a second, about $0.60). The workflow runs `--sync` first,
   which copies `operator_preview` into `model_registry.spec`.
2. Read-only check in the SQL editor. Expected result: both rows `hidden`, `verified_at` set, `operator_preview` true.
   `select id, availability, verified_at, spec -> 'operator_preview' as preview from public.model_registry where id in ('runway-gen4.5', 'runway-gen4-turbo');`
3. Sign in as the platform operator, in the operator's organization: the two models appear in the Studio marked "Preview",
   with "Powered by Runway" under the price key.

## Not verified

- The words of Runway's insufficient-credits answer: they are not on its error page. Third-party pages disagree, and the status may
  be 400, 402 or 412. The adapter maps a 4xx that says "not have enough credits" or "insufficient credits" to `quota`, and a 402 is
  already `quota`. The first real empty-balance answer should be checked against this.
- That `cost.credits` is present on a finished task for these models. The API reference lists the field, but no task has been run.
- The probe's real cost. The figures above are the pricing page's.
