### What changed

- **Registry** (`runway-gen4.5`, `runway-gen4-turbo`): `operator_preview: true`; `api_documented` points at the API reference of
  this date; pricing restates the page with `as_of` 2026-11-13; `terms_notes` gained the training licence, the Enterprise
  alternative, the age rule, the photo-consent rule, the embargo and the attribution requirement. `durations_s` stays 5 and 10.
  The upscaler (`runway-video-upscale`) is not part of the trial and is unchanged.
- **`pricing.confirmed` stays `false`.** In this repository `true` is kept for a price a probe's billed amount has matched
  (`docs/research/registry-corrections-2026-11-11.md`); no registry entry is `true` yet. The figure is the official page's, with its
  `source_url` and `as_of`. The Runway probe now prints Runway's own `cost.credits` beside OK, so the match can be checked.
- **The gen4.5 probe is image-to-video now** (it was text-to-video): the cheapest valid call the feature needs, 5 s of the 1280x720
  frame `tools/probe_models.py` draws, at 16:9 (Runway bills by the second, not by the ratio; 16:9 sends that frame uncropped).
- **The adapter** (`modules/capabilities/video.py`, `RunwayAdapter`) already matched the endpoint, the version header and the six
  ratios. Fixed: a length outside 2 to 10 whole seconds is refused before any call (it used to be sent); `ASSET.INVALID` is
  `bad_request` (it was the retryable `unavailable`, so the same unusable picture would have been paid for again); a 4xx whose body
  names a credit shortfall is `quota`, not "change your input"; THROTTLED is pinned as still running; a status the docs do not list
  is logged and kept pending.
- **The Studio** shows a model's `attribution` with a link: beside the price key in the panel, every desk and the overview composer,
  and on the job card of what that model made or is making. Before this the line appeared only on the Models catalog. The words are
  Runway's and are not translated; the spoken label around them is in en, ru and uz.

### MiniMax in the Studio: the gap, closed by 0149

Migration 0139 prices an operator preview with no credit price at the vendor's cost **only when the registry states one flat
figure**. Every MiniMax H3 entry is priced by resolution (`pricing.variants`), and 0139 answers such a preview `unpriced` ("has no
credit price yet"). So `operator_preview` was left off every MiniMax entry; listing them would only have shown a model whose every
generation is refused. The Runway entries have one flat figure and are not affected.

Migration 0149 (its own pull request, because it replaces a money function) lets a preview take the vendor cost of the quoted
resolution from `pricing.variants.prices`, in the same audited `creative_price` path: the request's resolution, else the model's
pinned `default_resolution` (768p for all four) when the request names none. A resolution with no figure of its own is refused
`unpriced`, never priced at another resolution's figure. The conversion is 0139's (1 credit = 1 US cent of vendor cost, no margin), no
credit price is written, and who may preview is unchanged (the platform operator, in the operator's own credits-exempt organization).
The same pull request puts `operator_preview` on H3, H3 Lite, H3 Highspeed and H3 Max. Hailuo 2.3 stays unmarked: MiniMax bills it per
clip, the registry states no per-second figure for it, and a preview of it could only answer `unpriced`.

What the operator will see (list prices, estimates; `pricing.confirmed` is still false):

| Model | Studio (no resolution sent: 768p) | 480p (only a request that names it; the Studio has no picker) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| H3 Lite | 4 credits a second (16 for 4 s) | 2.5 credits a second (10 for 4 s) |
| H3 | 8 credits a second (32 for 4 s) | not offered (H3 lists 768p and 2k) |
| H3 Highspeed | 6 credits a second (24 for 4 s) | 4 credits a second (16 for 4 s) |
| H3 Max | 8 credits a second | 5 credits a second |

Each quote is floored at `job_minimum`, like every quote. The exempt organization holds and pays nothing; the figure is the job's record.

**A second gap, found while testing 0149 and not changed by it.** The database's parameter check (`creative_params_problem`, last
replaced in 0126) accepts a named resolution only from 480p, 540p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p and 2160p. A request that names `768p` or `2k`
is refused `invalid_params` before any model is read. The Studio has no resolution picker and sends none, so the Studio trial runs at
768p, the pinned default, and works. H3's 2k cannot be asked for at all yet, and neither can a named 768p. The fix is a separate
migration on `creative_params_problem`; the lab test pins the gap as a strict xfail, so it reports the day the gap is closed.

## Before a sale (none of this is done here)

1. **Training on uploads.** Runway: a privacy-notice line saying Runway may train on what a person uploads and on the clips it
   makes (Terms §4.4), or the Enterprise Terms (§5.2), which forbid that. MiniMax: its ToS IP clause 3 lets it use input and
   output to improve its services, which needs the same privacy-notice line. The owner's choice.
2. **Photo consent.** Before an upload is sent, the person confirms in the UI that the photo is theirs, or that everyone in it
   agreed (Runway §4.2). A UI step in en, ru and uz, and a server-side record of the confirmation.
3. **Age rule.** Our user terms carry 13+, and under 18 only with a parent's or guardian's permission (Runway §2.2).
4. **Embargo.** Decide the `blocked_countries` list for Runway §18.12. The terms name none, so none is set.
5. **Price.** The owner sets the credit prices. The Runway probe's reported `cost.credits` is compared with 12 and 5 credits a
   second first, and MiniMax's usage page with the per-second figures; then `pricing.confirmed` is set and availability moves to
   `beta` in a reviewed change. A `credit_prices` row for the two Runway units may already exist from
   `docs/sql/prices_2026_10_01.sql`; check it, and do not assume it is the sale price.
6. **Attribution beyond the Studio.** The Library and an exported video do not carry "Powered by Runway" yet. A job card loses it
   once the model is no longer offered to that viewer, because the line is read from the offered models' spec. Recording it on the
   job's result would make it permanent.
7. **A refused input is still billed.** Runway does not refund `SAFETY.INPUT.*`; MiniMax does not say whether a failed task is
   billed. The job releases the person's hold, so the platform pays the vendor cost of every refused photo. Price that in, or screen
   inputs first.
8. **MiniMax revenue threshold.** The alert above, before any MiniMax image model is sold.

1. Merge. Then **Actions -> Probe models -> Run workflow**, one run per model (`model` = the id): `runway-gen4-turbo`,
   `runway-gen4.5`, `minimax-h3-lite`, `minimax-h3` (each one short clip at the registry's price). The workflow runs `--sync`
   first, which copies `operator_preview` and the gates into `model_registry.spec`. Each Runway OK line ends with
   `credits=<n>`, the figure Runway reported for the task: compare it with the registry estimate before `pricing.confirmed`.
2. Read-only check in the SQL editor. Expected: every row `hidden`, `verified_at` set; `preview` true for the Runway two only.
   `select id, availability, verified_at, spec -> 'operator_preview' as preview, spec -> 'terms_gate' as gate from public.model_registry where id in ('runway-gen4.5', 'runway-gen4-turbo', 'minimax-h3', 'minimax-h3-lite');`
3. Sign in as the platform operator, in the operator's organization: the Runway models appear in the Studio marked "Preview", with
   "Powered by Runway" under the price key. The MiniMax ones do not appear until migration 0149 (above).

For MiniMax, after the pull request with migration 0149 is merged (it sits on the one above, which is merged first):

4. Before it is applied, the read-only check in the 0149 file header (`creative_price` is 0139's body). Merging applies it as one
   transaction; its last block asserts the end state. Then the Verify query at the end of the file: every column true.
5. **Actions -> Probe models -> Run workflow** with `model` = `minimax-h3-highspeed` and `minimax-h3-max` if they were not

6. Read-only check: `preview` true for the two Runway and the four H3 entries, false for `minimax-hailuo-2.3`.
   `select id, availability, verified_at is not null as probed, spec -> 'operator_preview' as preview from public.model_registry where provider in ('runway', 'minimax') order by id;`
7. In the Studio, as the operator in the operator's organization: the H3 models are marked "Preview" and a 4 s clip of H3 Lite is
   quoted 16 credits (768p, the vendor's list price, no margin; floored at the job minimum). Nothing is charged: the organization is
