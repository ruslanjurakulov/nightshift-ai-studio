## Why they are not for sale, and what was and was not read

Read on 2026-10-25, each as markdown (append `.md`) on `platform.vidu.com/docs`: the Q3 text-to-video, image-to-video, start-end and reference-to-video references, the Q4 preview image-to-video and reference-to-video references, task detail, cancel, account balance, error codes, pricing, model map, FAQ, update notice, usage and limits, content moderation, and the Terms of Use (`terms-and-policies/terms-of-use`, "Effective on 2025-01-23", ShengShu AI HK Limited, Hong Kong; HKIAC arbitration, Hong Kong law).

**The one clause.** The Terms' headings are unnumbered on the page, but the page's own cross references fix the numbering (Prohibited Acts is "Clause 8", Contact "Clause 16"), which makes "Content Generated by End Users" clause 4. Its first paragraph is the permission: "you are entitled to integrate the API into your applications and authorize your end users to provide input such as prompts, texts, images, videos, or other similar materials to the Services ('Input'), and receive output from the Services". Its last paragraph is the open question: "International use must comply with all local laws, including sanctions and regulations. The output generated through using our service may not be sold, transferred, exported or re-exported to (a) any embargoed country or territory or (b) any individual or entity with whom dealings are prohibited or restricted ... You may not sell the Vidu-related technology directly or indirectly without obtaining the necessary approval from regulatory authorities and the prior consent of us." (An earlier research note placed this sentence under "Prohibited Acts"; on the page it sits at the end of clause 4.) Whether a credits product sells "the Vidu-related technology" is for Vidu to say: gate `terms_review_required`, and a person should ask platform@vidu.studio for a written confirmation.

Other clauses that bind a reseller: clause 4 "when you provide Vidu-related services to any third party, the corresponding obligations and liabilities shall be borne by you" and you "are obligated to inform your end users that they are responsible for their input"; clause 4 again, "You, as well as end users must not remove, falsify, cover the sign that can distinguish those output generated by using deep synthesis technology"; the same clause 4 bars selling, transferring or exporting output to an embargoed country or a restricted entity; clause 3 bars "the development of a competing products or services"; clause 8 bars "Misrepresentation" (claiming Output was made by a human), "Account Trading", "Competitive Use of Output" (developing competing models) and "Circumvention of Regional Restrictions" (proxies, VPNs); clause 2 says the account "should be a linked account via Google account service"; the Privacy clause makes an integrator "provide us with the list of the applications" and "apply the principle of data minimization". Payments go through Stripe. Age: "legal age to form a binding contract, or ... full authorizations from a validly existing legal entity".

The Terms say Input and Output may be monitored and "used in accordance with the privacy policy"; no sentence on training was found in the Terms, which is not proof there is none. ## What the adapter does

- `POST /ent/v2/text2video` | `img2video` | `start-end2video` answers `task_id` and `state`; `GET /ent/v2/tasks/{id}/creations` answers `state` (created, queueing, processing, success, failed), `err_code` and `creations[]` (`url`, `cover_url`, `watermarked_url`). One poll path serves every endpoint, so the task id alone is enough. The key is sent as `Authorization: Token <key>`. Redirects are never followed on any call.
- **Every link is valid for 24 hours** ("Creation URL, valid for 24 hours"): the file is stored by the `fetch` that runs straight after the poll that says `success`. A finished task must not be parked.
- **Concurrency:** "Each organization can use up to 5 concurrent tasks", per organisation and not per key, with the rest queued in submission order. That organisation is Nightshift's one Vidu account, so the 5 are shared by every customer; the registry's `max_concurrent_per_org` (Nightshift's own organisation) stays 1.
- `duration` and `resolution` are required and sent as given; the vendor's defaults (5 s, 720p) are not what a person was quoted. Q4 preview takes `2K` and `4K` upper case on the wire (the registry says `2k`, `4k`). `audio` is sent only when the job names it (the Q3 pricing rows have no audio column; the Q2 section adds 15 credits for audio, so a Q3 surcharge is not excluded: a probe should compare `credits` in the submit answer with and without). `off_peak` (half price, up to 48 h, cancelled and refunded if late) is never sent.
- **Image-to-video always sends `is_rec: false`.** On Q4 preview the vendor's default is `true`: the system writes a prompt, ignores the one given, and the pricing page bills "10 credits for every 5 prompts output".
- Pictures go inline as base64 and are read by their first bytes (PNG, JPEG, WebP), never by suffix; at most 7 MB each so two frames stay under a 20 MB body and the decoded size under the stated 10 MB. Start and end frame must have close resolutions (ratio 0.8 to 1.25) and an aspect ratio under 1:4: not checked locally, the vendor refuses.
- Every submit whose answer never came back (timeout, dropped connection, any 5xx, an unreadable 2xx, no usable `task_id`) raises with `not_accepted` False and a message that starts with `UNCONFIRMED SPEND`; the worker neither retries it nor moves it. A vendor error name is honoured only for 4xx (a gateway 503 can stand in front of a Vidu that took the task). Every failure after Vidu said the work was done (a refused link, a bad file) starts with `SPEND MAY HAVE OCCURRED`. The pricing page's claim that failed and moderated tasks "do not consume credits" is quoted as a vendor claim, never repeated as fact.
- **Result host: none.** The docs print placeholders and the only Vidu hosts on any page read are `image01.cf.vidu.studio` and `scene-aliyun.vidu.zone`, both for sample media. `RESULT_HOSTS` is empty, so every link is refused (fail closed): the first real probe fails with `SPEND MAY HAVE OCCURRED ... 'the host it saw'`, and a person puts that host in `modules/capabilities/vidu.py` once it is checked to be Vidu's. Pin the EXACT host (as `luma.py` does), never a parent domain such as `amazonaws.com.cn`, then run a NEW probe: **the first probe's spend is lost** (no code path fetches a finished task again; the job fails at once and the clip is not stored).
- **Watermark:** the adapter stores `url`, not `watermarked_url` (`STORE_WATERMARKED`). The file keeps Vidu's default metadata label (no `meta_data` is sent). Whether that satisfies the deep-synthesis clause is an owner decision not yet taken.
- **Not wired:** reference-to-video (`viduq3`, `viduq3-mix`, `viduq3-turbo` with subjects, `viduq4-preview`): the registry has no reference-to-video capability, and the subject library, voice ids and 1-15 reference images have no request field; `viduq3-ad`, `viduq3-drama`; Q2, Q1 and Vidu 2.0; audio, image, lip-sync, motion-sync, scene-template and one-click products; callbacks; cancel. The docs list no text-to-video or start-end endpoint for Q4 preview, and Pro Fast has a start-end page but no start-end price, so neither offers an end frame.
- The estimate uses a prompt cap of 2000 characters (ours): the Q3 pages say 20000 (Turbo text-to-video: 5000), an update notice of 2025-09-17 says 2000, the FAQ says about 1500 for legacy models. Raise it after a probe.
- Pro's image-to-video page describes its own `model` field as `viduq3-pro-fast` while its example sends `viduq3-pro`: a copy error on Vidu's page; the example is followed.

## Prices (all ESTIMATES, `confirmed: false`)

Credits per output second, from `overview/pricing`: Pro 540p 9 / 720p 20 / 1080p 24; Turbo 7 / 12 / 13; Pro Fast 720p 12 / 1080p 15; Q4 preview 540p 9 / 720p 19 / 1080p 24 / 2K 38 / 4K 78 (Q3 reference rows are not wired). **The USD per credit is on no page read.** The docs price a credit at 0.03125 RMB; at an assumed 7.2 RMB per USD that is $0.0043, rounded up to $0.005 here. At that rate: Pro $0.045 / $0.10 / $0.12, Turbo $0.035 / $0.06 / $0.065, Pro Fast $0.06 / $0.075, Q4 preview $0.045 / $0.095 / $0.12 / $0.19 / $0.39 per second. The international console may price credits differently. The quality and speed tiers are judgement, not a benchmark.

## To enable one (owner)

1. Settle the terms: ask Vidu (platform@vidu.studio) for a written statement that selling generations for credits through an application is allowed; read the privacy policy; decide the watermark question.
2. Set the repository secret `VIDU_API_KEY` (never in chat); set a spend cap. The key page has no key check: the key is never sent anywhere by it.
3. `python tools/probe_models.py --dry-run --model vidu-q3-turbo`, then the real probe by name: about $0.07 for Turbo (2 s at 540p, start and end frame), $0.09 for Pro, $0.14 for Q4 preview (3 s at 540p). The first probe is expected to fail on the unknown result host and its spend (the amounts above) is lost. Read the host off it, pin that EXACT host in `modules/capabilities/vidu.py` (never a parent domain such as `amazonaws.com.cn`), then run a NEW probe.
4. Read the USD price of a credit off the console, re-check the table, set `confirmed: true`, add the `credit_prices` rows, clear `terms_gate` in a reviewed change.
