## What the adapter does

Sources, all read 2026-10-25: `dev.pika.art/llms.txt`, `dev.pika.art/openapi.json`, the operation pages `dev.pika.art/llms/pika/{pika-2.5/text-to-video, pika-2.5/image-to-video, pikaframes/image-to-video}` and their catalogue records (`?expand=inputs`). The `llms.txt` carries an "Agent Workflow" section addressed to AI agents; it was read as page data and not followed.

- `POST /v1/media/pika/<model>/<function>` answers a job `{"id", "status"}`; `GET /v1/media/jobs/{id}` answers `status` (queued, running, completed, failed), `output.video.url` and, on failure, `error {code, message}` and `billing`. A submit rejected AFTER the job row exists (no balance, rate limit, unpriceable input) answers the failed job envelope, with a 403 / 429 / 503 status, so the adapter reads `error.code` from the body of a 4xx and from a 2xx; for a 5xx the status decides and the failure stays UNCONFIRMED.
- Key in `X-API-Key`. The key goes to the API host and nowhere else; no redirect is followed on any call. `Idempotency-Key` is not sent (a lost answer is never retried, so there is nothing for it to protect).
- `resolution` and `duration_s` are required and sent as given (Pika's resolution default is 1080p, its dearest). Pika 2.5 text-to-video is 5 s only. Image-to-video also takes 10 s, but the catalogue prices 1080p at $0.09 per second for a 5 s clip and $0.06 for its generic tier, so only 5 s is offered and the 1080p figure is exact. Pikaframes is one transition between two pictures, 1-5 s (the page says 1-10 but caps "multiple transitions" at 5 s each, and the cap for two pictures is not stated). No aspect ratio exists on these operations, and no sound flag (`audio_out: false`: no page names sound).
- **Pictures are URLs, never bytes** (`image` and `images` must match `^https?://`). A local picture is first uploaded: `POST /v1/media/uploads` with the raw bytes and their real type, the OpenAPI document's "legacy CDN URL (raw request)" form, which answers `{"url"}` directly. The documented form is a presigned PUT to a storage host the pages do not name; the adapter does NOT follow a vendor-supplied upload URL. The picture is read by its first bytes (PNG, JPEG, WebP), never by suffix, at most 20 MB, and before anything is sent. The hosted `url` is checked against Pika's hosts before it is put in the paid call. The upload is not billable, so every failure of it is reported as not accepted; once it has gone out, the creative bridge treats a later refusal as unproven (it only trusts a submit's first call).
- Every submit whose answer never came back (timeout, dropped connection, any 5xx, an unreadable 2xx, no usable `id`) raises with `not_accepted` False and a message that starts with `UNCONFIRMED SPEND`; no retry, no other model. Every failure after Pika said the work was done starts with `SPEND MAY HAVE OCCURRED`. "Only successful generations are charged" is Pika's claim, quoted as one; a failed job's message adds the charge Pika's own job record reports, labelled as Pika's statement, when `billing.state` is `settled`.
- **Result hosts:** `api.dev.pika.art` (the page's example is `https://api.dev.pika.art/v1/files/video_....mp4`) and `cdn.pika.art` (hosted media in the examples), exact hosts, not `pika.art`. The pages do not say whether the files route needs the key. The key is NEVER sent to a result link, so if a probe's download answers 401 / 403 it fails closed with the spend note and a person decides. How long a link or the job's media lives was not found: store the clip at once. `DELETE /v1/media/jobs/{id}` and `DELETE /v1/media/uploads?url=` erase the stored media and the request; they are not called (deleting after storing is an option for reducing what Pika retains).
- Limits: "a per-minute or per-day request limit, or the org's job queue is full" (429, `Retry-After`); no numbers were found. The registry says 1 concurrent job per Nightshift organisation.

## Not done

- **Pikaffects** (`pikaffects/image-to-video`, 61 effects, $0.05 per request; `pikaffects/video-to-video`, 9 effects), **Pikaswaps** and **Pikadditions** (`video-to-video`, $0.03 per request each): the registry has no capability that carries a NAMED effect, and `video_edit` is priced per source second in the database while Pika charges per request; a video must be uploaded by the presigned form (the raw upload lists image and audio types only), whose storage host the pages do not name; and Pikaswaps' region (ROI text or a mask image) and Pikadditions' reference image have no request field. They need a schema decision, not an adapter.
- Image-to-video of 10 s, loop and 3-5 keyframes with per-transition prompts, the audio, merge, compose and caption operations (`pika-audio/*`, `video-merge`, `video-compose`, `dynamic-captions`), webhooks, and all 168 resold operations.

## Prices (all ESTIMATES, `confirmed: false`)

Catalogue `sell_usd` per output second, read 2026-10-25 from the public catalogue and each operation page: Pika 2.5 720p $0.04, 1080p $0.09 at 5 s (the generic 1080p tier is $0.06); Pikaframes 720p $0.04, 1080p $0.06. A 5 s Pika 2.5 clip is therefore $0.20 at 720p and $0.45 at 1080p. `POST /catalog/apis/{api_id}/quote` is free and would give the exact figure per request; it was not called (it needs a key). The quality and speed tiers are judgement, not a benchmark.

## To enable one (owner)

1. Settle the terms (the three questions above), and decide where "Powered by Pika" shows.
2. Set the repository secret `PIKA_API_KEY` (never in chat), add prepaid funds (generation fails with `insufficient_balance` otherwise), set a spend cap. The model-keys page checks the key with the free balance read.
3. `python tools/probe_models.py --dry-run --model pika-pikaframes`, then the real probe by name: $0.04 (Pikaframes, 1 s at 720p) or $0.20 (Pika 2.5, 5 s at 720p). The probes go through image-to-video, so they also prove the upload.
4. Re-check the prices (use `/quote`), set `confirmed: true`, add the `credit_prices` rows, clear `terms_gate` in a reviewed change.
